- **Type**: `{ρ hρ0 hρ1} {ε} {x y} (hx) (hy) {I J} {c} (hcx : coeff I x = c) (hc0 : (c : hatK) ≠ 0) (hcnorm : Valued.v c = gaussNormRPS … x) (hIJ : I ≤ J) (htail : ∀ K, degLex.toSyn I < degLex.toSyn K → Valued.v (coeff K x) ≤ ε * gaussNormRPS … x) : ∃ z : ↥(ArSub …), v z * ‖x‖ ≤ ‖y‖ ∧ (tail bound for coeff of X^{J-I}z·x above J) ∧ ‖y - X^{J-I}z·x‖ ≤ ‖y‖ ∧ (coeff J of the difference is 0 or has strictly smaller degAr than c)`
- **What**: **The single division step** underlying Kedlaya's Lemma 3.8: divide the `J`-th coefficient of `y` by the leading coefficient `c` of `x` and subtract the resulting monomial multiple, obtaining norm non-increase, an `ε`-tail bound transported from `x` to the product, and a strict degree drop at `J`.
- **How**: The coefficient division comes from the project's `exact_division` (Kedlaya Prop. 2.9 in `Euclidean.lean`) applied to `c` and `coeff J y`, giving `z ∈ A^r` with `v(coeff J y - z·c) ≤ v(coeff J y)` and the degree drop.
