### `theorem teichCoeff_frob`
- **Type**: `(x : Ainf p F) (n : ℕ) : teichCoeff p F (frob p F x) n = teichCoeff p F x n ^ p`
- **What**: The Witt Frobenius `φ` on `A_inf = W(O_F)` acts on Teichmüller coordinates coefficient-wise by the `p`-th power: the `n`-th Teichmüller coordinate of `φ x` is the `p`-th power of the `n`-th coordinate of `x`.
- **How**: Unfold `teichCoeff`; in characteristic `p` the Witt Frobenius is `WittVector.map` of the ring Frobenius, so `(frob p F x).coeff n = (x.coeff n)^p` by `frobenius_eq_map_frobenius` + `WittVector.map_coeff` + `frobenius_def`; then `map_pow` moves the power through the coordinate map.
- **Hypotheses**: `F` perfectoid of characteristic `p` (so `frob` is the char-`p` Witt Frobenius equiv).
