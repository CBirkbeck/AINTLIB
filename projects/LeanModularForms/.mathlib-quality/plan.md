## Strategy (post expert review — NO q-expansion principle / modular curves)
Reduce both to ONE atomic input **(FIH)**: the integral Hecke algebra `𝕋ℤ ⊆ End_ℂ(S_k(Γ₁N))`
(ℤ-subalgebra generated by all `T_n`, `U_p`, diamonds) is a **finite ℤ-module**. Then:
- **(A)**: `a_n(f) = λ_f(T_n) ∈ O_f := im(λ_f : 𝕋ℤ → ℂ)`, module-finite over ℤ ⟹ integral.
- **(B)**: `ℚ ⊗ O_f` is a finite-dim ℚ-domain ⊆ ℂ ⟹ a field ⊇ `K_f`.

FIH is justified by **(IHR)**: a finite integral Manin-symbol module + integral Hecke action +
an **injective** Eichler–Shimura period map `S_k ↪ H_ℤ ⊗ ℂ`. IHR is research-scale (not in mathlib);
it is the project's single deep residual.
