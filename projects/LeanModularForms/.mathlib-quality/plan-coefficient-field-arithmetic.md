# Plan: Hecke-eigenvalue arithmetic (the 2 `Labels/NewformOrbit.lean` deep sorries)

**Date**: 2026-06-19, **revised 2026-06-20 after expert review** (`.mathlib-quality/expert-review/2026-06-19/`).
**Branch**: `dev/leanmodularforms`.

## Targets
- `coeffSeq_isIntegral`: each `a_n(f)` of a `Newform N k` is an algebraic integer.  **(A)**
- `instFiniteDimensionalCoeffField`: `K_f = ℚ(a_n : n)` is a number field.  **(B)**

## Verdict from review (supersedes the original q-expansion-principle plan)
We do **NOT** need the q-expansion principle or an integral model of `X₁(N)`.  The original
candidate — an integral lattice *inside* `S_k(Γ₁N)` — is both stronger than necessary and **wrong as
stated** (on a character space the `T_n` coefficient formula carries `χ(d)`, a root of unity ∉ ℤ, so
`{f : a_n ∈ ℤ}` is not `T_n`-stable over ℤ; it is an `O_{ℚ(χ)}`-lattice).  The right route builds a
finite integral Hecke module **elsewhere** (modular symbols / parabolic cohomology) and maps `S_k`
into it.

## Layered plan

### Layer 0 — the atomic interface: (FIH)
> **(FIH)**  `𝕋^full_{k,N,ℤ} ⊆ End_ℂ(S_k(Γ₁N))`, the image of the ℤ-subalgebra generated by **all**
> classical Hecke operators (`T_n` for `(n,N)=1`, `U_p` for `p|N`, and the diamonds `⟨d⟩`), is a
> **finite ℤ-module**.

This is exactly what (A),(B) consume; it commits the library to neither curves nor symbols.

### Layer 1 — derive (A),(B) from (FIH)  [PROVABLE NOW, ~150–250 LOC]
- The eigensystem `λ_f : 𝕋^full_ℤ → ℂ`, `T ↦ (eigenvalue on f)`, is a ring hom; `O_f := im λ_f` is
  module-finite over ℤ (quotient of the finite ℤ-module `𝕋^full_ℤ`).
- **(A)**: `a_n(f) = λ_f(T_n) ∈ O_f`; every element of a module-finite ℤ-algebra is integral over ℤ.
  *(mathlib: `IsIntegral` of elements of a `Module.Finite` algebra; `Algebra.IsIntegral.of_finite`.)*
- **(B)**: `ℚ ⊗ O_f` is a finite-dim ℚ-subalgebra of `ℂ`, a domain, hence a field ⊇ `K_f`;
  `[K_f:ℚ] ≤ rank_ℤ 𝕋^full_ℤ`.  *(mathlib: a finite-dim algebra over a field that is a domain is a
  field.)*
- Plumbing: connect `coeffSeq f` / `Eigenform.eigenvalue` to `λ_f(T_n)` (the codebase already has the
  `a_n ↔ eigenvalue` bridge on character spaces).

### Layer 2 — (FIH) from (IHR), the integral modular-symbol realization  [the deep input]
> **(IHR)**  A finitely generated free `ℤ`-module `H_{k,N}` with integral actions of `T_n, U_p, ⟨d⟩`,
> and an **injective**, Hecke-equivariant `ι : S_k(Γ₁N) → H_{k,N} ⊗_ℤ ℂ`.

Then `𝕋^full_ℤ` = image of the integral Hecke algebra in `End_ℤ(H_{k,N}) ≅ M_r(ℤ)`, a subgroup of a
finite free ℤ-module, hence finite — giving (FIH).  (Only **injectivity** of `ι` is needed, not the
full Eichler–Shimura isomorphism.)

**The connection modular symbols ↔ modular forms = the Eichler–Shimura period pairing.**
For `k ≥ 2`, let `V_{k-2,ℤ} = Sym^{k-2}(ℤ²)` (degree-`(k-2)` homogeneous polynomials in `X,Y`, with
the `SL₂` action).  The **integral modular-symbol module** `𝕄_k(Γ₁N,ℤ)` is the `Γ₁N`-coinvariants of
`Div⁰(P¹(ℚ)) ⊗ V_{k-2,ℤ}` (paths `{α,β}` between cusps, twisted by `P ∈ V_{k-2}`); equivalently its
dual is `H¹_par(Γ₁N, V_{k-2,ℤ})`.  The **period pairing**
```
⟨ f , {α,β} ⊗ P ⟩  =  ∫_α^β  f(z) · P(z,1) · dz        (f ∈ S_k, P ∈ V_{k-2}),
```
converges (cusp forms decay exponentially at the cusps), is `Γ₁N`-invariant (the weight-`k`
automorphy of `f` and the `Sym^{k-2}` action on `P` cancel along `γ`, so the pairing descends to
`𝕄_k(Γ₁N)`), and is **Hecke-equivariant** (`⟨T_n f, m⟩ = ⟨f, T_n m⟩`).  It realizes `S_k(Γ₁N)` as a
Hecke-submodule of `𝕄_k(Γ₁N,ℤ)^∨ ⊗ ℂ` — i.e. the injection `ι`.  Take `H_{k,N} = 𝕄_k(Γ₁N,ℤ)^∨_tf`
(or `H¹_par(...)_tf`).

**Decomposition of (IHR):**
- **IHR-a — the integral modular-symbol module (Manin presentation).**  `𝕄_k(Γ₁N,ℤ)` is finite free
  over ℤ with the **Manin presentation**: generators = Manin symbols `(g, P)`, `g ∈ Γ₁N\SL₂ℤ` (a
  finite coset set, ↔ `P¹(ℤ/N)`), `P ∈ V_{k-2,ℤ}`; relations `x|(1+S)=0` (`S` order 2) and
  `x|(1+U+U²)=0` (`U=TS` order 3), plus the boundary/parabolic relation.  Purely combinatorial/
  algebraic.  *(mathlib has `groupCohomology`, `Representation`, symmetric powers; modular symbols
  themselves must be built.)*
- **IHR-b — integral Hecke action on `𝕄_k`.**  `T_n`, `U_p`, `⟨d⟩` act by finite sums of integer
  matrices (Heilbronn / Hecke matrices on Manin symbols).  Combinatorial.
- **IHR-c — the period pairing is well-defined, Γ-invariant, Hecke-equivariant.**  The genuine
  **analytic** input: convergence (codebase cusp-form decay at cusps + mathlib contour integrals),
  the cocycle/coinvariance computation, and matching the two Hecke actions.
- **IHR-d — injectivity of `ι`** (a cusp form with all periods zero is zero).  The "easy half" of
  Eichler–Shimura, but still a real analytic statement (e.g. via non-degeneracy of the period /
  Petersson pairing).

### Cheaper (B)-only branch
For **(B) alone**, the *rational* version (RHR) suffices: a finite-dim ℚ-space `H_ℚ` (Betti
cohomology — **no rational form of `S_k` itself needed**) + injective Hecke-equivariant
`S_k ↪ H_ℚ ⊗ ℂ` ⟹ `𝕋_ℚ` finite-dim ⟹ `K_f` a number field.  Once the Manin module is built over ℚ,
going to ℤ is a modest extra cost; the hard analytic input (IHR-c/d) is shared.

### Caveats (from review Q6)
- **k ≥ 2** uniform (`Sym^{k-2}ℤ²`; `k=2` → trivial coeffs ℤ).  ✓ our target.
- **k = 1** is NOT covered (no `Sym^{-1}`); needs Deligne–Serre / geometric — out of scope.
- **`U_p` / bad primes are essential for (A)**: a `T_n`-only (`(n,N)=1`) argument gives integrality
  only of the *good* coefficients; the recursions don't manufacture `a_p` from good eigenvalues.
- **torsion**: small `N` → torsion in `H`; quotient by torsion (Hecke preserves it, ℂ kills it).
- **nebentypus**: globally on `S_k(Γ₁N)` no difference; per-`χ` the integral ring is `O_{ℚ(χ)}`.

## Recommended order
1. **Layer 1** — (A),(B) from an *abstract* (FIH) interface (provable now; cleanly isolates the input
   and discharges both sorries modulo (FIH)).
2. **IHR-a, IHR-b** — the algebraic modular-symbol module + integral Hecke action (combinatorial).
3. **IHR-c, IHR-d** — the period pairing + injectivity (the hard analytic core; the irreducible
   classical input).  Decide here whether to fully formalise or isolate (IHR)/(FIH) as a cited input.

- **build**: the modular-symbol module + Manin presentation; integral Hecke (Heilbronn) action; the
