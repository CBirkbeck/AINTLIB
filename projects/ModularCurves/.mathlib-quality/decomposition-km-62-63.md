## 0. What the gate proves (the consumer)

The Main Theorem 6.1.1 needs `D = G^×` (the divisor register `D` of KM 6.1 equals the scheme of
generators `G^×`). Both are finite over `S`, so `D ↪ G^×` is a closed immersion; the content is that it
is an ISOMORPHISM. KM proves it by a **variant of the homogeneity argument of §5.2**: pass to the
universal case via two moduli problems `𝒫₁, 𝒫₂` (triples `(P,G,Q)`, `Q ∈ D(S)` resp. `Q ∈ G^×(S)`),
with `𝒫₁ → 𝒫₂` (since `D ⊆ G^×`), and show this morphism is an isomorphism.

### [HOMOG-1] 6.2.1 Axiomatic Isomorphism Theorem — NEW ★, the top result
**Verbatim (p.155–156):** "Let `𝒫₁` and `𝒫₂` be moduli problems on (Ell), and `𝒫₁ → 𝒫₂` a morphism
between them. Suppose that (1) Both `𝒫₁` and `𝒫₂` satisfy axioms Reg. 1, Reg. 3, and Reg. 4A of (5.2).
(2) After inverting `p`, the induced morphism … is an isomorphism `𝒫₁⊗ℤ[1/p] ≅ 𝒫₂⊗ℤ[1/p]`. (3) For any
algebraically closed field `k` of characteristic `p`, and any supersingular elliptic curve `E₀/k` with
universal formal deformation `E/W(k)[[T]]`, the morphism of finite `W[[T]]`-schemes `𝒫₁,E → 𝒫₂,E` is an
isomorphism. Then the given morphism is an isomorphism `𝒫₁ ≅ 𝒫₂`."
**Proof (verbatim):** "entirely analogous to … the Axiomatic Regularity Theorem 5.2.1 … interpret the
finite `M(δ)`-schemes as coherent sheaves of algebras `𝓕₁, 𝓕₂` … a `C_{M(δ)}`-linear map `𝓕₂ → 𝓕₁` …
consider the kernel and cokernel as coherent sheaves `0 → Ker → 𝓕₂ → 𝓕₁ → Coker → 0`. Then `V` is
precisely the open set of the noetherian scheme `M(δ)` over which the coherent sheaf `Ker ⊕ Coker`
vanishes."
**Map:** NEW. Needs the §5.2 infra (Reg axioms, `M(δ)` auxiliary-level covers, the 5.2.1 open-set
argument). **PREREQUISITE-CHECK (feasibility make-or-break): is the §5.2 Axiomatic-Regularity machinery
present in-project?** If YES, HOMOG-1 mirrors it; if NO, that machinery is a prior gate (large). The
coherent-sheaf `Ker ⊕ Coker` vanishing on an open containing all points is: a f.g. module over a
noetherian ring that is 0 at every prime (support empty) ⟹ 0 — mathlib `Module.support_eq_empty_iff`
(HAVE, used in FLAT1). "Iso off a closed set + iso at every point ⟹ iso" is the shape.
**Attacks:** (1) children/parent: the theorem reduces an iso-of-moduli to iso-at-(1/p)-points + iso-at-
supersingular-points; the open-cover-by-Ker⊕Coker-support is the genuine content. (2) source: verbatim
6.2.1. (3) discharge: hinges on the §5.2 prerequisite — flag as the first feasibility gate.

### [HOMOG-4] 6.3.4 mult-by-Q injective on A₁ — NEW ★ (the analytic crux)
**Verbatim (p.159–160):** "The endomorphism 'multiplication by Q' on `A₁` is injective. Proof. By
Weierstrass preparation, we may view `A₁` as the quotient of the polynomial ring `A[Q]` by the monic
polynomial in `Q` `∏_{b mod pⁿ,(b,p)=1}(Q-[b](P)) = 0`. Therefore `A₁` is `A`-free with basis
`1,Q,Q²,…,Q^{φ(pⁿ)-1}`. Because `A` is a regular local ring, it is an integral domain, so an endomorphism
of a free `A`-module of finite rank is injective if and only if its determinant is non-zero in `A`. The
determinant of 'multiplication by Q' on `A₁` is `±` the constant term of the monic polynomial …
`det_A(Q|A₁) = ∏_{b mod pⁿ,(b,p)=1}[b](P)`. To see that this determinant is non-zero … There is an obvious
action of the group `(ℤ/pⁿℤ)ˣ` on the moduli problem `[Γ₁(pⁿ)]`, given modularly by `P ↦ bP`. The induced
action of `(ℤ/pⁿℤ)ˣ` on `A` is given precisely by `P ↦ [b](P)`. Therefore the determinant is the product
of `P` and all its transforms by a group of automorphisms of `A`. As `P` is non-zero in `A`, being part of
a regular system of parameters … all its `(ℤ/pⁿℤ)ˣ`-transforms are also non-zero, and hence, `A` being a
domain, their product is non-zero in `A`."
**Map:** NEW. Weierstrass preparation over a complete local ring (SURVEY mathlib: `PowerSeries` /
`WeierstrassPreparation`?), free-module-endomorphism `injective ⟺ det ≠ 0` over a DOMAIN (mathlib
`LinearMap.det`, `Matrix.mulVec` injectivity via det in an integral domain — likely HAVE or easy),
determinant of mult-by-Q on `A[Q]/(monic)` = ± constant term (companion-matrix det — standard), the
`(ℤ/pⁿℤ)ˣ`-action transporting `P` to `[b](P)`, `A` regular local ⟹ domain (mathlib `IsRegularLocalRing`
⟹ `IsDomain`, HAVE).
**Attacks:** (1) the det = constant-term-of-monic is the companion-matrix determinant; the nonzero-ness is
the homogeneity heart (regular params + group action). (2) verbatim 6.3.4. (3) `LinearMap.det` on a free
module + `IsDomain` gives the injective⟺det≠0; Weierstrass prep is the one to survey.

### [HOMOG-5] 6.3.6 formal-group multiplication — NEW (uses [a](X)=aX mod X²)
**Verbatim (p.161–162):** "Let `a` be an integer. In the ring `A` we have `[a](P) = P × (unit of A)` if
`(a,p)=1`, `P × (elt. of max A)` if `p | a`. Proof. Consider the series `[a](X)` expressing 'multiplication
by a' on our formal group `Ê` over `A`: `[a](X) = aX mod (X²)A[[X]]`; therefore `[a](X) = X × (a + elt. in
XA[[X]])`. Substituting `P ∈ max(A)` gives the assertion."
**Map:** NEW. Formal-group `[a](X) = aX mod X²` (mathlib formal-group-law API — the leading-coefficient
fact; SURVEY), `P ∈ maximal ideal`, unit ⟺ `a` unit ⟺ `(a,p)=1` (in `W`, `a` unit ⟺ `p∤a`).
**Attacks:** (1) elementary once `[a](X)=aX mod X²` is available; `a+elt-in-max` is a unit ⟺ `a` a unit.
(2) verbatim 6.3.6. (3) needs the formal-group `[a]`-series API.

### [HOMOG-6] 6.3.5 A₂/QA₂ ≅ A₁/QA₁ — NEW (coeff-ideal comparison, uses HOMOG-5)
**Verbatim (p.160–161):** "The natural map `A₂/QA₂ → A₁/QA₁` is an isomorphism. Proof. … `A₂/QA₂ = A/K̲`
where `K̲` is the ideal of `A` generated by the coefficients of the polynomial `X^{pⁿ} - ∏_{a mod pⁿ}(X-[a](P))`.
`A₁/QA₁ = A/b̲` where `b̲` is the principal ideal generated by `∏_{b mod pⁿ,(b,p)=1}[b](P)`. We must show
`K̲ = b̲`. … `K̲ ⊂ b̲` [from `D ⊂ G^×`]. To reverse … the element `∏_{(b,p)=1}[b](P)` lies in `K̲`: consider
the term of degree `pⁿ - φ(pⁿ)` in `X^{pⁿ} - ∏(X-[a](P))`. Its coefficient lies in `K̲`. … up to a unit in
`A`, this coefficient is equal to `∏_{(b,p)=1}[b](P)` [= the sum of all `φ(pⁿ)`-fold products of distinct
elements of `{[a](P) : a mod pⁿ}`]."
Then (concluding, p.162): "both quantities `∏_{(b,p)=1}[b](P)` and the coefficient of `X^{pⁿ-φ(pⁿ)}` are of
the form `(unit in A) × P^{φ(pⁿ)}`" — via HOMOG-5.
**Map:** NEW. Elementary-symmetric-polynomial coefficients of `∏(X-[a](P))` (mathlib `MvPolynomial.esymm` /
`Polynomial.prod_X_sub_C_coeff`), the `K̲ ⊂ b̲` from surjectivity, the reverse via the `X^{pⁿ-φ(pⁿ)}`
coefficient = elementary symmetric = `(unit)·P^{φ(pⁿ)}` (HOMOG-5 applied termwise).
**Attacks:** (1) the coefficient = elementary symmetric function of the `[a](P)`; each `[a](P) = P·(unit or
max)` (HOMOG-5) ⟹ the `φ(pⁿ)`-fold product terms are `(unit)·P^{φ(pⁿ)}` — matching `b̲`. (2) verbatim.
(3) needs `esymm` coefficient identities.

### [HOMOG-7] 6.3.3 assembly — NEW (Nakayama + snake)

**Map:** NEW. Nakayama (mathlib `RingTheory/Nakayama.lean`, HAVE — used in FLAT1/the depth work), the snake
lemma (mathlib `CategoryTheory`/homological-algebra snake, HAVE), `Ker` f.g. over `A[[Q]]` (noetherian).
**Attacks:** (1) standard homological assembly given 4+6. (2) verbatim 6.3.3. (3) the snake lemma applied
to `×Q` needs the setup as `ShortComplex`/module maps.
