## 0. Route decision — read the ACTUAL Stacks proofs, then decide

The prior make-or-break (projective-dimension / syzygy-freeness) was refuted by the airtight
counterexample `R=k[t], S=k[t,x], M=S/(xt)` (first syzygy `(xt)≅S` free everywhere, yet `M` is not
`R`-flat on `V(t)`). What controls `R`-flatness is fibre-**EXACTNESS** of the resolution, not
freeness of a syzygy. Any leaf reducing flatness to syzygy-freeness is WRONG and is rejected here.

I transcribed the source proof chain of **Stacks 00RC (Theorem 10.129.4)** and each sub-result. The
verdict on the strategic question ("does the target need full 00N1, or does Ext-support suffice?"):

> **FULL Buchsbaum–Eisenbud (00N1) IS REQUIRED. The "lean via Ext-support" idea is real and useful
> but does NOT eliminate 00N1 — it only replaces one sub-step of 00RB (openness of the depth
> condition, Stacks 10.129.2) with an Ext-support argument.**

Decisive evidence — the **actual** 00RB proof (Lemma 10.129.3), transcribed:

> "The proof aims to show openness using Proposition 10.102.9 [=00N1]... it defines `I_i ⊂ S` as
> the ideal generated by `r_i × r_i`-minors of `φ_i`. Proposition 10.102.9 characterizes exactness:
> the complex `F̄_{•,𝔮}` is exact **if and only if** for each `1 ≤ i ≤ e`, either `(I_i)_𝔮 = S_𝔮`
> or `(I_i)_𝔮` contains a `S_𝔮/𝔭S_𝔮`-regular sequence of length `i`."

Both directions of 00N1 are used: (⟹) exact ⟹ rank+depth conditions (to know the conditions hold at
the flat point `𝔮`); (⟸) conditions-near-`𝔮` ⟹ exact-near-`𝔮` (the HARD acyclicity, to conclude
fibre-exactness on the neighbourhood). Ext-support gives openness of the *depth condition* only —
it cannot supply the (⟸) acyclicity implication.

**Why no cheaper route exists (three dodges, all refuted):**

1. *Constructibility instead of openness.* The flat locus is generisation-stable
   (`flatLocus_stableUnderGeneralization`, HAVE), so by Stacks 00I0 constructible ⟺ open **given**
   generisation-stability. Constructibility is therefore exactly as hard as openness — no shortcut.
2. *Constructibility from generic flatness + Noetherian induction.* Refuted by the counterexample:
   over the stratum `V(t)⊂Spec R`, `M⊗κ` is `R/(t)=k`-flat (everything is), but that is NOT
   `R`-flatness at those points. Generic-flatness-over-strata builds the WRONG set.
3. *00MI-only, avoiding 00RB openness.* 00MI gives flatness POINTWISE from fibre-exactness; it does
   not give a neighbourhood. Bootstrapping openness still needs fibre-exactness to be open = 00RB =
   00N1. (An exact free complex, localised so the top syzygy is free, still does **not** split when
   `M` is not `R`-flat — the counterexample again — so no splitting dodge.)

**Leaner-than-Stacks findings (banked wins, NOT dodges):**

- **00MI is Tor-free and B-E-free** (Lemma 10.99.5, transcribed: proof is "induction on `e`... base
  case invokes Lemma 10.99.1 [00ME]", *no* 00MK, *no* commutative Tor). So the flatness-from-fibre-
  exactness half is elementary — matching the Tor-free style of our existing `LocalCriterion`.
- The whole assembly is **Tor-free**: we never need mathlib commutative `Tor`. We use 00MI (resolution
  form) in place of 00MK (Tor form).
- mathlib has **more** than the 2026-07 survey found (see §2): `Module.freeLocus` openness,
  `Module.support_eq_zeroLocus`, full derived `Ext`, `HasProjectiveDimensionLE`↔Ext, and crucially
  `RingTheory.Sequence.IsWeaklyRegular.of_flat_of_isBaseChange` — a direct mirror of Stacks 10.129.2
  (fibre-regular sequences persist under flat base change), the exact engine 00RB needs for the
  depth-openness sub-step. So Stacks' native (regular-sequence) route to 00RB's openness is already
  well-supported; the Ext-support alternative is optional.

## 5. Feasibility verdict

- **Tractable now (no research risk):** [T-DEVISSAGE], [T-ME], [T-MI], [T-REDUCEP], [T-FINAL], and the
  HAVE inputs (00LP, 00MH, HilbertSyzygy, freeLocus). These assemble existing AINTLIB + mathlib pieces
  and are Tor-free. Combined ~1100–1900 LOC of standard work.
- **Substantial but standard API build:** [T-FIT] (Fitting/minor ideals — a real gap, but classical
  and self-contained) and [T-GRADE] (grade packaging + openness; mathlib's regular-sequence-under-flat
  base change does most of the openness work). ~700–1200 LOC.
- **Genuine multi-week+ research formalization — the single blocker:** **[T-BE] = Stacks 00N1
  Buchsbaum–Eisenbud acyclicity**, whose hard (⟸) direction (BE.3) is the acyclicity theorem
  (McCoy-rank + depth induction on complex length). It is ABSENT from mathlib together with all of its
  Fitting-ideal/minor-rank scaffolding. It is **not** a dead monolith — it decomposes (BE.1 McCoy rank,
  BE.2 easy half, BE.3 acyclicity induction) into buildable sub-tickets — but BE.3 is the irreducible
  core with no known shortcut, and §0 shows the Ext-support optimisation does **not** avoid it.

**Bottom line:** the STATEMENTS all typecheck (skeleton builds, 8616 jobs) and the route is
source-faithful and complete. The residual's difficulty is concentrated in one named theorem —
**00N1 (Buchsbaum–Eisenbud)** — which is a multi-week+ formalization in its own right; the Tor-free /
Ext-support ideas make everything *around* it tractable but cannot remove it. Recommend attempting
[T-FIT] → [T-GRADE] → [T-BE] first as the make-or-break, landing the tractable Tor-free leaves in
parallel.
