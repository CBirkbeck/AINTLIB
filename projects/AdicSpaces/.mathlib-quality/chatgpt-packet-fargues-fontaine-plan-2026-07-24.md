## Setting and goal

The primary source is Definition 2.1.1 of Birkbeck–Feng–Hansen–Hong–Li–Wang–Ye, *Extensions of
vector bundles on the Fargues–Fontaine curve* (arXiv:1705.00710):

> Let E be a finite extension of Q_p, with uniformizer π, ring of integers E°, and residue field
> F_q where q = p^f, and let F/F_q be an algebraically closed perfectoid field, with ring of
> integers F° and pseudouniformizer ϖ. Let W_{E°}(F°) = W(F°) ⊗_{W(F_q)} E° be the ramified Witt
> vectors of F° with coefficients in E°. Define 𝒴_{E,F} = Spa(W_{E°}(F°)) \ {|p[ϖ]| = 0}, and let
> φ : 𝒴 → 𝒴 be the Frobenius automorphism induced by the natural q-Frobenius φ_q = φ^f ⊗ 1 on
> W_{E°}(F°). The (mixed-characteristic) adic Fargues–Fontaine curve is 𝒳_{E,F} = 𝒴_{E,F}/φ^ℤ.

Supporting sources: Scholze–Weinstein *Berkeley Lectures* §12.2, §13.1, Def 13.5.1 (A_inf =
W(O_{C♭}) with the (p,[p♭])-adic topology; κ∘φ = pκ; "φ acts properly discontinuously on
𝒴_(0,∞) ... so it makes sense to form the quotient"); Kedlaya, *Sheaves, stacks, and shtukas*
(AWS 2017 notes) §3.1, especially Remark 3.1.9, quoted verbatim:

> Suppose that R is Tate, and let ϖ ∈ R be a pseudouniformizer. ... Y_S is the subspace of
> v ∈ Spa(A_inf, A_inf) for which v(p[ϖ]) ≠ 0. This space can be covered by the subspaces
>   U_n := {v ∈ Y_S : v(p)^{cp^n} ≤ v(ϖ) ≤ v(p)^{p^n}},
>   V_n := {v ∈ Y_S : v(p)^{p^{n+1}} ≤ v(ϖ) ≤ v(p)^{cp^n}}   (n ∈ ℤ),
> where c ∈ (1,p) ∩ Q is arbitrary. The action of φ permutes the U_n (among themselves) and the
> V_n (among themselves), and hence is properly discontinuous. The spaces U_0 and V_0 map
> isomorphically to their images in X_S and cover the latter.

(In these inequalities v(ϖ) means v([ϖ]), the Teichmüller lift; exponents like cp^n are rational
and are interpreted by clearing denominators, which is legitimate because valuations are
multiplicative and [ϖ^n] = [ϖ]^n.)

## The plan (please attack it)

Scope decision 1: formalize the case **E = Q_p** only (so W_{E°}(F°) = W(F°), q = p, φ_q = the
Witt Frobenius). Ramified Witt vectors do not exist in mathlib and would be a separate large
project. The general-E curve is deferred; the file layout is chosen so it can slot in later.

Scope decision 2: generalize F from "algebraically closed perfectoid" to **any perfectoid field
of characteristic p** (with chosen pseudouniformizer ϖ ∈ O_F, a topologically nilpotent unit of
F). Kedlaya's Remark 3.1.9 and SW's construction only need Tate + perfectoid; algebraic
closedness is used later (classification of bundles, classical points), which is out of scope.

Scope decision 3 (the big one): this campaign constructs 𝒳 as a **topological space with a
well-behaved quotient structure**, plus all the point-set theorems that make "𝒴/φ^ℤ" honest,
namely the covering, proper discontinuity, freeness, open quotient map, local injectivity
(each U_n, V_n maps homeomorphically onto its open image in 𝒳), quasicompactness of 𝒳. The
structure presheaf on 𝒳 (and sheafiness, Kedlaya's noetherian adic space theorem) is explicitly
a FOLLOW-ON campaign. Question for you: is this an honest reading of "define the adic FF curve",
or is the presheaf so essential that the campaign is mislabeled without it?

Proposed Lean-level construction (E = Q_p; F perfectoid field char p; O_F := power-bounded
subring; ϖ a pseudouniformizer with [ϖ] := Teichmüller lift in A := W(O_F)):

   reduce to mathlib's p-adic completeness of W(O_F) + ϖ-adic completeness of O_F levelwise;
   alternatively the coefficientwise argument (an I-adic Cauchy sequence has each Teichmüller/

   KGE q v :⇔ v([ϖ])^b ≤ v(p)^a   ("κ(v) ≥ q")   and   KLE q v :⇔ v(p)^a ≤ v([ϖ])^b.
   Totality of the valuation order gives KGE q v ∨ KLE q v for every q. Windows
   U_n := {KGE (p^n) ∧ KLE (c·p^n)}, V_n := {KGE (c·p^n) ∧ KLE (p^{n+1})} with c := (p+1)/2.
   Claims (all provable by pure ordered-abelian-group manipulations, using only
