### Statement (Phase 1)

`cyclo_elems_mem_cycloUnits` is a **theorem** stating the following:

Let `p` be prime, `p ≠ 2`, and let `a` be a natural number not divisible by `p`. For
every level `n ≥ 1`, the `n`-th component of the Coleman-input cyclotomic-unit tower
`c(a) = (c_n(a))_n` — namely `c_n(a) = (ξ_{p^n}^a − 1)/(ξ_{p^n} − 1)` viewed as a unit
of `ℂ_[p]ˣ` — lies in the project's **cyclotomic-unit group** `𝒟_n = cycloUnits p n`.
Membership in `𝒟_n` is, by definition, two things at once: (1) membership in the
subgroup of `ℂ_[p]ˣ` *generated* by `{±ξ_{p^n}} ∪ {ξ_{p^n}^a − 1 : 1 ≤ a ≤ p^n − 1}`
(the set `cycloGenSet`), and (2) membership in the global units `𝒱_n = globalUnits p n`
(integral over `ℤ` with integral inverse, lying in `F_n = ℚ(μ_{p^n})`).

Mathematically: *the cyclotomic unit `c_n(a)` is a cyclotomic unit in the textbook
sense* — it equals the explicit word `(ξ^{a mod p^n} − 1)·(ξ − 1)⁻¹` in the named
generators (this is the "subgroup generated by" half), and it is a unit of the ring of
integers `𝒪_{F_n}` (the "global" half). This is RJW TeX 3084: "the cyclotomic units
`c_n(a)` … are naturally elements of `𝒟_n`, hence global."

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[hp : Fact p.Prime]` — the prime `p`.
- `ℂ_[p]` — the `p`-adic complexes (the fixed ambient field for the whole development).
- `n : ℕ` — the cyclotomic level (conductor exponent).

Hypotheses (Lean side):
- `ha : ¬ (p : ℕ) ∣ a` — `a` is prime to `p` (so `c_n(a)` is a unit / coprime exponent).
- `hp2 : p ≠ 2` — the odd-prime hypothesis carried throughout RJW §11 (needed to package the tower `cyclo`).
- `hn : 1 ≤ n` — positive level (at `n = 0` the formula `0/0` is junk; the tower is set to `1` there).

Conclusion (math): the cyclotomic unit `c_n(a)` is a cyclotomic unit of `F_n` (lies in the generated subgroup) and is globally integral with integral inverse.

Conclusion (Lean): `(cyclo p ha hp2).elems n ∈ cycloUnits p n`.

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: **the group of cyclotomic units (circular units)** of `F_n`,
prime-power level — Washington `C_n`, Sinnott/Washington `Cyc(F) ∩ U(F)`. The specific
elements `c_n(a) = (ξ^a − 1)/(ξ − 1)` are, for `(a,p)=1`, **exactly the standard
generators** of this group at prime-power conductor.

Sources agree on the standard form: **yes**. Every source (Wikipedia, Washington Ch. 8,
Sinnott, Encyclopedia of Mathematics, the arXiv literature) defines the cyclotomic-unit
group as *(the subgroup generated by `±ζ` and the `(ζ^a−1)/(ζ−1)`) ∩ (the unit group)*
at prime-power level. The project's `cycloUnits := Subgroup.closure (cycloGenSet) ⊓ globalUnits`
is a faithful, verbatim transcription of this.

Most general standard form: for an arbitrary abelian field `F` of conductor `f`, the
group is generated by `±1` and norms `N_{ℚ(ζ_d)/ℚ(ζ_d)∩F}(1 − ζ_d)` over divisors `d ∣ f`,
intersected with the units (Sinnott). At prime-power conductor this collapses to the
`(ζ^a−1)/(ζ−1)` generators the project uses.

Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
  - **conductor**: from prime-power `p^n` (the project's case) to arbitrary `f` (Sinnott's general circular units). The most general is arbitrary `f`.
  - **ambient field**: the literature works in the abstract number field `F` / `ℚ(ζ_f)`; the project embeds everything in `ℂ_[p]` (a deliberate architecture choice so the global units and their `p`-adic closures share one ambient — see the parent `cycloUnits` report, question 5).

Disagreement with the literature: **none on the statement**. The *membership* asserted
here — that `c_n(a)` is a cyclotomic unit — is **definitionally trivial in the
literature** (`c_n(a)` is literally one of the named generators, and its global
integrality is the standard "these are units of `𝒪_{F^+}`" fact). The non-triviality in

the exponent `a` mod `p^n` to land in the `1 ≤ · ≤ p^n − 1` generator range; matching the
junk-guarded tower value `(cyclo).elems n` to the explicit word via `Units.ext`).
