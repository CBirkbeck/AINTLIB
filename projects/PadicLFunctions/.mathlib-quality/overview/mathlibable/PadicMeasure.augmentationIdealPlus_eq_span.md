### Statement (Phase 1)

`PadicMeasure.augmentationIdealPlus_eq_span` is a **theorem** stating the following:

Let `p` be an odd prime and let `𝒢 = ℤ_p^×` act with `a` a topological generator (one whose image generates `(ℤ/p^n)^×` at every finite level `n`). Write `𝒢⁺ = ℤ_p^× / {±1}` and `ā` for the image of `a` in `𝒢⁺`. Then the **augmentation ideal** `I(𝒢⁺)` of the Iwasawa algebra `Λ(𝒢⁺)` is **principal**, generated by `[ā] − [1]`:
$$ I(\mathcal G^+) \;=\; \bigl([\bar a] - [1]\bigr)\,\Lambda(\mathcal G^+). $$
Mathematically it is the **transport of the principality theorem for `Λ(𝒢)` along the natural surjection** `π_* : Λ(𝒢) ↠ Λ(𝒢⁺)`: since `deg⁺ ∘ π_* = deg`, every degree-0 measure on `𝒢⁺` lifts to a degree-0 measure on `𝒢`, where principality (the sibling `augmentationIdeal_eq_span`, RJW Lem. 3.38) is already known, and `π_*` carries the generator `[a]−1` to `[ā]−1`.

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[hp : Fact p.Prime]` — the residue characteristic.
- `(hp2 : p ≠ 2)` — `p` is odd (needed so `{±1}` is a non-trivial quotient and the `𝒢⁺` machinery applies).
- `{a : ℤ_[p]ˣ}` — a unit, intended as a topological generator of `𝒢 = ℤ_p^×`.

Hypotheses (Lean side):
- `(ha : ∀ n : ℕ, Subgroup.zpowers (unitsToZModPow p n a) = ⊤)` — `a` topologically generates: its image generates the finite cyclic-ish group `(ℤ/p^n)^×` at every level `n`. (This is the project's spelling of "topological generator of `ℤ_p^×`".)

Conclusion (math): the augmentation ideal of `Λ(𝒢⁺)` is the principal ideal generated by `[ā] − 1`.

Conclusion (Lean):
`augmentationIdeal p (G := GPlus p) = Ideal.span {(dirac p (QuotientGroup.mk a : GPlus p) - 1 : PadicMeasure p (GPlus p))}`.

**The underlying definitions are all project-local** (no mathlib counterpart):
- `PadicMeasure p X := C(X, ℤ_[p]) →ₗ[ℤ_[p]] ℤ_[p]` — the Iwasawa algebra `Λ(X)` realised as the continuous `ℤ_p`-dual (RJW Def. 3.6). `Measure/Basic.lean:52`.

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: **the augmentation ideal of a (completed) group ring `Λ(G) = ℤ_p[[G]]`** — `I(G) = ker(deg : Λ(G) → ℤ_p)` — together with its **principality for a (pro-)cyclic group**: when `G` is topologically generated by one element `g`, `I(G) = (g − 1)·Λ(G)`. The specific result here is the **functorial transport of this principality along a quotient surjection** `Λ(G) ↠ Λ(G/H)` (here `H = {±1}`, `G/H = 𝒢⁺`).

Sources agree on the standard form: **yes**. Universally: aug. ideal `= ker(augmentation)`, generated by `{g−1}`; principal exactly when one generator suffices, i.e. (pro-)cyclic `G`. For `Λ(Γ)` with `Γ ≅ ℤ_p` the textbook normal form is `Λ(Γ) ≅ ℤ_p[[T]]`, `T ↦ γ−1`, so the augmentation ideal is `(T) = (γ−1)`.

Most general standard form: for any `R` and any group `G` with a closed normal subgroup `H` such that `G/H` is topologically (pro-)cyclic generated by `ḡ`, the relative augmentation ideal `ker(Λ(G) → Λ(G/H))`'s image, and the augmentation ideal of `Λ(G/H)` itself, is principal generated by `ḡ−1`. The transport along `Λ(G)↠Λ(G/H)` is standard (Venjakob; the Hopf-quotient picture on nLab).

Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
  - Coefficient ring: from `ℤ_p` up to any commutative `R` (the augmentation-ideal-as-`ker` statement holds over any `R`; principality needs the cyclic structure).
  - Group: from the very specific `ℤ_p^×/{±1}` here, to any topologically pro-cyclic compact group, to (for the bare "generated by `{g−1}`" statement) any group.
  - Completion: the bare group ring `R[G]` vs. the **completed/Iwasawa** algebra `ℤ_p[[G]]` (an inverse limit). The completion is what makes this analytic Iwasawa theory rather than plain algebra.

Disagreement with the literature: **none**. The Lean statement is a faithful special case of the standard "augmentation ideal of a (completed) (pro-)cyclic group ring is principal, generated by `(generator) − 1`", transported across the quotient `𝒢 ↠ 𝒢⁺`.
