## Phase 1 — Comprehend

### Statement (Phase 1)

```lean
lemma mem_zetaIdeal_iff (hp2 : p ≠ 2) {x : PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ} :
    x ∈ zetaIdeal p hp2 ↔ ∃ l ∈ augmentationIdeal p (G := ℤ_[p]ˣ),
      algebraMap (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ) (QuotientField p) x
        = algebraMap (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ) (QuotientField p) l * padicZeta p hp2 :=
  Iff.rfl
```
with `variable (p : ℕ) [hp : Fact p.Prime]`.

`mem_zetaIdeal_iff` is **a theorem stating** the following (read mathematically):

A measure `x ∈ Λ(ℤ_p^×)` lies in the ideal `I(𝒢)ζ_p` **iff** there exists an element `l`
of the augmentation ideal `I(𝒢) = ker(deg)` such that, in the total fraction ring
`Q(𝒢) = Frac(Λ(ℤ_p^×))`, the image of `x` equals `l · ζ_p`. That is: membership in
`I(𝒢)ζ_p` unfolds to "`x` is, up to the embedding `Λ ↪ Q`, of the form `l·ζ_p` with `l`
in the augmentation ideal."

**Crucially**, the right-hand side is **verbatim** the defining `carrier` of the parent
`def zetaIdeal` (`ZetaGalois.lean:254–257`):
```lean
def zetaIdeal (hp2 : p ≠ 2) : Ideal (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ) where
  carrier := {x | ∃ l ∈ augmentationIdeal p (G := ℤ_[p]ˣ),
    algebraMap (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ) (QuotientField p) x
      = algebraMap (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ) (QuotientField p) l * padicZeta p hp2}
  add_mem' := …; zero_mem' := …; smul_mem' := …
```
So `x ∈ zetaIdeal p hp2` is **definitionally** `x ∈ carrier`, i.e. the RHS predicate;
hence the proof is `Iff.rfl`.
