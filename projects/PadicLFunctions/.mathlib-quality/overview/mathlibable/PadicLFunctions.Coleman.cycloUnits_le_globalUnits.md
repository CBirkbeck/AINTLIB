Let `p` be a prime and `n : ℕ`. Inside the field `ℂ_[p]` of `p`-adic complex
numbers, the **cyclotomic units** `𝒟_n` (`cycloUnits p n`) form a subgroup of

`𝒟_n ≤ 𝒱_n`: every cyclotomic unit is a global unit. Mathematically this is the
trivial half of the cyclotomic-unit setup ("cyclotomic units are units of the
ring of integers") — it holds *by construction* here, because `𝒟_n` was defined
by intersecting with `𝒱_n`.
