### Statement (Phase 1)

`PadicMeasure.zetaIdealPlus_eq_span` is **a theorem giving the principal-ideal (computational)
description of `I(𝒢⁺)ζ_p`**, the even-part instance of the right-hand side of Iwasawa's Main
Conjecture:

> Let `Λ(𝒢⁺) = Λ(ℤ_p^×/{±1})` be the even-part cyclotomic Iwasawa algebra (here the algebra of
> `ℤ_p`-valued measures `C(𝒢⁺, ℤ_p) →ₗ[ℤ_p] ℤ_p`, RJW Def. 3.6), `Q(𝒢⁺)` its total ring of
> fractions, `I(𝒢⁺) = ker(deg)` its augmentation ideal, and `ζ_p⁺ ∈ Q(𝒢⁺)` the descended
> Kubota–Leopoldt p-adic zeta pseudo-measure (`padicZetaPlus`, RJW Cor. 11.4). Let `a ∈ ℤ_p^×` be
> a topological generator (`∀ n, ⟨unitsToZModPow p n a⟩ = ⊤`) and let `ν ∈ Λ(ℤ_p^×)` be the
> 𝒢-side witness of `([a]−1)·ζ_p` (i.e. `([a]−1)·ζ_p = ν` in `Q(𝒢)`). Then the ideal
> `I(𝒢⁺)ζ_p := { x : ∃ l ∈ I(𝒢⁺), toQPlus x = toQPlus l · ζ_p⁺ }` of `Λ(𝒢⁺)` is **principal**,
> generated by the pushed-forward witness `π_*(ν)`:
> `I(𝒢⁺)ζ_p = ( π_*(ν) )`.

This is the 𝒢⁺-side twin of `zetaIdeal_eq_span` (line 276 of the same file, the 𝒢-side
`I(𝒢)ζ_p = (ν)`). The proof is a genuine two-direction `le_antisymm`: (⊆) lifts the witness

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: the **principality of `I(𝒢⁺)ζ_p`** — "the ideal of the (even-part) Iwasawa
algebra generated by the augmentation-ideal-times-pseudo-measure is principal, generated by a single
witness `([γ₀]−1)ζ_p` at a topological generator `γ₀`". `I(𝒢⁺)ζ_p` itself is the **even-part
right-hand side of Iwasawa's Main Conjecture** ((+)-type / totally-real side). Equivalent names: "the
ζ_p-ideal", "the characteristic-ideal side of the (+)-MC", "the principal ideal of the Stickelberger /
p-adic L pseudo-measure".
Sources agree on the standard form: **yes** — RJW arXiv:2309.15692 (§11.2, Prop. TeX 3052, plus the
augmentation-ideal principality Lem. 3.38), Coates–Sujatha *Cyclotomic Fields and Zeta Values* (Ch. 4),
Ouyang's and nLab's MC statements (characteristic ideal generated by one element via a topological
generator). The principality is uniform: the augmentation ideal `I(G)` is the (topologically)
principal ideal `([γ₀]−1)`, so `I(G)ζ_p = ([γ₀]−1)ζ_p` is principal; on the even part `G⁺` the same
statement is asserted "similarly".
Most general standard form: for **any** profinite (commutative `p`-adic Lie) group `G` admitting a
topological generator (or, more precisely, where the augmentation ideal is principal) and a
pseudo-measure `ζ ∈ Q(G)`, the ideal `I(G)ζ` is principal, generated by `([γ₀]−1)ζ`. The cyclotomic
even part `G⁺ = ℤ_p^×/{±1}` is the principal case here; the 𝒢-side `G = ℤ_p^×` is the twin
(`zetaIdeal_eq_span`).
Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
  - **the group `G`**: from the concrete even part `ℤ_p^×/{±1}` (and its twin `ℤ_p^×`) to an arbitrary
    profinite group with principal augmentation ideal. The Lean theorem fixes `G⁺ = GPlus p`; the
    project *already* states the **identical** result for the twin group `G = ℤ_p^×` (`zetaIdeal_eq_span`),
    which is itself strong evidence that the general-`G` form is the natural target.
  - **the pseudo-measure `ζ`**: from the specific descended Kubota–Leopoldt `ζ_p⁺` to an arbitrary
    pseudo-measure `q ∈ Q(G)`. The principal-ideal computation works for any such `q` with a witness.
Disagreement with the literature: **none**. The Lean form is a faithful, correctly-stated
specialisation/instance of the standard "`I(G)ζ_p` is principal".
