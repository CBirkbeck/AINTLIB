### Statement (Phase 1)

`PadicMeasure.zetaIdeal_eq_span` is **a theorem** stating the following:

> Let `p` be an odd prime, `Λ(𝒢) = Λ(ℤ_p^×)` the cyclotomic Iwasawa algebra (realised here
> as `ℤ_p`-valued measures `C(ℤ_p^×, ℤ_p) →ₗ[ℤ_p] ℤ_p`, RJW Def. 3.6), `Q(𝒢) = Frac Λ(𝒢)`
> its total ring of fractions (RJW Def. 3.34), `I(𝒢) = ker(deg)` the augmentation ideal
> (RJW Def. 3.37), and `ζ_p ∈ Q(𝒢)` the Kubota–Leopoldt p-adic zeta pseudo-measure (RJW
> Def. 4.10). Fix a *topological generator* `b` of `ℤ_p^×` (an element whose image generates
> `(ℤ/p^n)^×` for every `n`), and let `ν ∈ Λ(𝒢)` be the witness measure for `b` — i.e. `ν`
> represents the product `([b]−[1])·ζ_p`, which lies in `Λ(𝒢)` by the pseudo-measure
> property. Then the ideal `I(𝒢)ζ_p` of `Λ(𝒢)` is **principal, generated by `ν`**:
> `I(𝒢)ζ_p = (ν) = ([b]−[1])·ζ_p · Λ(𝒢)`.

This is the "computational form" of `I(𝒢)ζ_p`: the abstract carrier definition (`x = l·ζ_p`,
some `l ∈ I(𝒢)`) is shown to coincide with a single principal generator. The mathematical
engine is the **principality of the augmentation ideal** `I(𝒢) = ([b]−[1])` (`augmentationIdeal_eq_span`,
RJW Lem. 3.38), pushed through the multiplication by `ζ_p` in the fraction ring: since every
`l ∈ I(𝒢)` is `([b]−1)·ρ`, every `l·ζ_p` is `ρ·(([b]−1)·ζ_p) = ρ·ν`, so the ideal is `(ν)`.

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[hp : Fact p.Prime]` — the prime.
- `hp2 : p ≠ 2` — odd prime; essential, because both `ζ_p` and a topological generator of
  `ℤ_p^×` require it (`(ℤ/8)^×` is not cyclic). Enters via `padicZeta p hp2`/`zetaIdeal p hp2`.
- implicit `{b : ℤ_[p]ˣ}` — the topological generator.
- implicit `{ν : PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ}` — the witness measure for `b`.

Hypotheses (Lean side):
- `hb : ∀ n, Subgroup.zpowers (unitsToZModPow p n b) = ⊤` — `b` is a topological generator.
- `hν : algebraMap … (dirac p b − 1) * padicZeta p hp2 = algebraMap … ν` — `ν` represents
  `([b]−[1])·ζ_p` (the existence of such a `ν` is exactly the pseudo-measure property of `ζ_p`).

Conclusion (math): `I(𝒢)ζ_p` is the principal ideal `([b]−[1])·ζ_p · Λ(𝒢)`.

Conclusion (Lean): `zetaIdeal p hp2 = Ideal.span {ν}` — an equality of `Ideal (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ)`.
