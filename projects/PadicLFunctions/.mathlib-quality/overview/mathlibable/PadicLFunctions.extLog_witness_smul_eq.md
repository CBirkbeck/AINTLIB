### Statement (Phase 1)

`PadicLFunctions.extLog_witness_smul_eq` is **a theorem** stating the following:

Let `L` be a complete ultrametric normed field that is a normed `ℚ_p`-algebra.
Fix `x ∈ L`. Suppose `x` admits **two** "rational-valuation witnesses": positive
integers `m, m'`, integers `k, k'`, and ball elements `y, y'` (each with
`‖y − 1‖, ‖y' − 1‖` inside the exponential ball, hence of norm `1`) such that

  `x^m = p^k · y`   and   `x^{m'} = p^{k'} · y'`.

Then the two candidate values for the extended logarithm agree:

  `(1/m)·log_p(y) = (1/m')·log_p(y')`   (scalars in `ℚ_p`, `log_p = padicLog`).

In words: **the formula `m⁻¹·log_p(y)` defining `extLog x` does not depend on which
witness `(m, k, y)` of the equation `x^m = p^k·y` you pick** — the well-definedness
obligation underlying the `def extLog`. The proof raises each witness to the
other's exponent (`x^{mm'}`), matches `p`-valuations using `‖y‖=‖y'‖=1` and the
injectivity of `n ↦ (p⁻¹)^n`, deduces `y^{m'} = y'^m`, applies `log_p(z^n) = n·log_p z`
to get `m'·log_p y = m·log_p y'`, and divides by `m·m'`.

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[Fact p.Prime]` — the residue characteristic.
- `L : Type*`, `[NormedField L]`, `[NormedAlgebra ℚ_[p] L]`, `[IsUltrametricDist L]`, `[CompleteSpace L]` — a complete nonarchimedean field over `ℚ_p`. (The `ℚ_p`-algebra assumption is the carrier of the `(m : ℚ_[p])⁻¹` scalar — see Phase 4.)

Hypotheses (Lean side):
- `hm : 0 < m`, `hm' : 0 < m'` — the exponents are positive (so `m⁻¹` is meaningful).
- `hxy : x ^ m = (p:L)^k * y`, `hxy' : x ^ m' = (p:L)^k' * y'` — the two witnesses.
- `hy : InExpBall p (y − 1)`, `hy' : InExpBall p (y' − 1)` — the ball members of each witness (this forces `‖y‖ = ‖y'‖ = 1`).

Conclusion (math): the witness-scaled logarithm `(1/m)·log_p(y)` is well-defined — independent of the witness.

Conclusion (Lean): `((m : ℚ_[p]))⁻¹ • padicLog p y = ((m' : ℚ_[p]))⁻¹ • padicLog p y'`.

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: **the well-definedness of the Iwasawa-extension of the
`p`-adic logarithm** — i.e. that `log_p(x) := (1/n)·log_p(x^n)` (for `n` with
`x^n ∈ p^ℤ·(1+𝔪)`) is independent of `n`.

Sources agree on the standard form: **yes**. Two equivalent standard packagings:
  - *Concrete recipe* (Washington §5.1; Iwasawa; MIT 18.785): pick any `n` with `x^n`
    in the good subgroup, set `log_p(x) = (1/n)log_p(x^n)`; well-defined because the
    target `C_p` (resp. `ℚ_p`) is a `ℚ`-vector space.
  - *Bundled homomorphism* (Tate p-divisible groups; Coleman/Perrin-Riou): `log_p` is
    *the* unique continuous group homomorphism into the uniquely-divisible additive
    group, extending the power-series log with `log_p(p)=0`; well-definedness is then
    a corollary of the universal property of extension into a divisible target.

Most general standard form: `log_p` as the unique continuous `MonoidHom` (mult. group
→ additive group of a complete nonarchimedean `CharZero` field) extending the
power-series log, normalised by `log_p(p)=0`; the kernel is `p^ℚ·μ`. The
target lemma is the value-level shadow of "this extension exists and is unique."

Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
  - **Base field**: literature states it over `C_p` (or any complete alg. closed /
    complete nonarchimedean `CharZero` field). The Lean form fixes a `ℚ_p`-algebra `L`
    — strictly narrower (the `ℚ_p`-algebra structure is only used to host the `(m:ℚ_p)⁻¹` scalar).
  - **Scalar field of the value**: literature divides by `n` inside the value's own
    field (`1/n ∈ C_p`). The Lean form takes `(m:ℚ_[p])⁻¹` and `• ` into `L` — i.e. it
    routes the division through `ℚ_p` rather than through `L` directly. Same artefact
    flagged on the parent `padicLog`.
  - **Packaging**: literature increasingly states it as the bundled-homomorphism /
    divisible-target universal property (modern idiom), not as a freestanding scalar
    identity (the Lean form).

Disagreement with the literature: **none on content** — the lemma is true and is the
standard well-definedness fact. The divergence is in *generality* and *packaging*:
the Lean form is a freestanding scalar identity over a `ℚ_p`-algebra, where the
literature's modern form is the (unique) extension of a homomorphism into a
uniquely-divisible target over a general complete nonarchimedean `CharZero` field.

**Rationale.**

This is the well-definedness lemma for the bespoke `extLog` construction: it certifies
that `extLog x := m⁻¹·log_p(y)` does not depend on the witness `(m,k,y)` of
`x^m = p^k·y`. The literature confirms the *content* is standard and canonical — it is
the well-definedness of Iwasawa's extension `log_p(x) = (1/n)log_p(x^n)`, true precisely
because the value lives in a uniquely-divisible target (Tate, Washington §5.1, MIT
18.785). So this is not novel mathematics. But it is also **not** `NO-mathlib-has-it` or
`NO-composable`: mathlib has no p-adic logarithm whatsoever (the `padicLog` identifier
occurs zero times in the entire mathlib source, confirmed by grep and Loogle), so neither
