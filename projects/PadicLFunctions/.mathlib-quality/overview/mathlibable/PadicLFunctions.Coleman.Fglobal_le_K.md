### Statement (Phase 1)

`Fglobal_le_K` is **a theorem** stating the following:

> Let `ζ = ξ_{p^n}` be a fixed primitive `p^n`-th root of unity in `ℂ_p`. Then the rational
> cyclotomic field `F_n = ℚ(ζ)` is contained, as a subset of `ℂ_p`, in the local cyclotomic
> field `K_n = ℚ_p(ζ)`. Equivalently: every element of `ℚ(ζ)` lies in `ℚ_p(ζ)`.

This is the elementary tower fact that the generated subfield is **monotone in the base
field**: since `ℚ ⊆ ℚ_p` (both inside `ℂ_p`) and the generator `ζ` is common to both, the
smallest field over `ℚ` containing `ζ` sits inside the smallest field over `ℚ_p` containing `ζ`.

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[hp : Fact p.Prime]` — the prime (fixes `ℚ_[p]`, `ℂ_[p]`).
- `n : ℕ` (implicit) — the cyclotomic level.
- `x : ℂ_[p]` (implicit) — the element being placed in both fields.
- Ambient: `ℂ_[p]` is a `CharZero` field, an algebra over `ℚ_[p]` and over `ℚ`, with the
  canonical scalar tower `ℚ → ℚ_[p] → ℂ_[p]`.

Hypotheses (Lean side):
- `hx : x ∈ Fglobal p n` — i.e. `x ∈ ℚ⟮ζ⟯`.

Conclusion (math): `x ∈ ℚ_p(ζ)`.
Conclusion (Lean): `x ∈ K p n`.

The current proof is a manual 5-case `IntermediateField.adjoin_induction` over `hx`:
- `mem` (the generator): `ζ ∈ K` by `zetaSys_mem_K`;
- `algebraMap` (rationals): `q ∈ K` by `SubfieldClass.ratCast_mem` (since `ℚ ⊆ ℚ_p ⊆ K`);
- `add` / `mul` / `inv`: `K` is a field, closed under these.
This is exactly the long-hand expansion of `adjoin_le_iff` (see Phase 6).

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: **the subfield of `ℂ_p` generated by a root of unity over a base field**
(`k(S)` / "field generated by `S` over `k`" / simple extension `k(α)`); and the elementary
**monotonicity of `k(S)` in the base** `k`. The specific instance `ℚ(ζ_{p^n}) ⊆ ℚ_p(ζ_{p^n})`
is standard background in cyclotomic Iwasawa theory.

Sources agree on the standard form: **yes.** `k(S)` is uniformly the smallest subfield
containing `k ∪ S` (Stacks 09FT Def 9.6.6; Dummit; nLab-adjacent notes). `F ⊆ K ⇒ F(S) ⊆ K(S)`
is the immediate consequence (the smaller `k` gives a smaller intersection-of-superfields).

Most general standard form: for fields `F ⊆ K` inside a field `L` and any `S ⊆ L`,
`F(S) ⊆ K(S)`. In mathlib terms: with a scalar tower `F → K → E`,
`IntermediateField.adjoin F S ≤ (IntermediateField.adjoin K S).restrictScalars F`.

Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
- base pair: literature states it for arbitrary `F ⊆ K`; our form fixes `(ℚ, ℚ_p)`.
- generating set: literature states it for arbitrary `S`; our form fixes the singleton `{ζ}`.
- ambient field: literature for any `L ⊇ K`; our form fixes `ℂ_p`.

Disagreement with the literature: **none.** The Lean statement is the literature fact,
specialised to `(F,K,E,S) = (ℚ, ℚ_p, ℂ_p, {ζ_{p^n}})`.

## Verdict: `Fglobal_le_K`

**Category:** `NO-composable-from-mathlib`

**Evidence:**
- Literature search (Phase 3): the inclusion is the textbook monotonicity of `k(S)` in the
  base field (Stacks 09FT Def 9.6.6: `k(S)` = smallest subfield ⊇ `k∪S`); the specific
  `ℚ(ζ) ⊆ ℚ_p(ζ)` is routine background in cyclotomic Iwasawa theory — no named theorem.
- Generality analysis (Phase 4): STRICTLY NARROWER — a `(ℚ, ℚ_p, ℂ_p, {ζ})` specialisation of
  the general base-change-monotonicity lemma; the general lemma is a *separate* contribution,
  not this declaration generalised.
- Mathlib search (Phase 5): found building blocks (`adjoin_le_iff`, `subset_adjoin`,
  `mem_restrictScalars`; mathlib's own `algebraicClosure.adjoin_le` is the identical idiom);
  composition would yield our form.
- Composition check (Phase 6): **COMPOSABLE** in ≤2 calls.

**Rationale.**
`Fglobal_le_K` is a one-direction inclusion of two simple extensions sharing a generator,
`ℚ(ζ) ⊆ ℚ_p(ζ)`. Mathlib already exposes the universal property that proves it in one line —
`IntermediateField.adjoin_le_iff` — together with `IntermediateField.subset_adjoin` (and the
project's `zetaSys_mem_K`, which is itself `subset_adjoin ℚ_[p] {ζ}`). The composition
`adjoin_le_iff.mp (adjoin_le_iff.mpr (singleton_subset_iff.mpr (zetaSys_mem_K p n))) hx`
reproduces the statement; mathlib even ships the *exact same idiom* for the algebraic-closure
analog (`algebraicClosure.adjoin_le := adjoin_le_iff.2 <| le_restrictScalars …`,
`FieldTheory/AlgebraicClosure.lean`). The current hand-rolled 5-case `adjoin_induction` proof
is the long-hand of what `adjoin_le_iff` discharges automatically. Nothing `p`-adic enters: no
norm, no completeness, no spectral input — only `ζ ∈ K` and that `K` is a field.

Because the declaration is the *specialisation* of a general lemma and follows from mathlib
primitives in ≤2 calls, mathlib should not carry this `ℚ_p`/`ℂ_p`/`zetaSys`-specific wrapper.
This does **not** mean delete it from the project: with `K = 5` internal uses and no inline
re-derivation, it is legitimate, reused project glue and should stay — just with its proof
golfed to the composition (a `/cleanup` action, not a `/mathlibable` one).

**WHY not — refactor-actionable detail (NO-composable-from-mathlib):**
Mathlib has the building blocks; `Fglobal_le_K` is a ≤2-call composition of them.
