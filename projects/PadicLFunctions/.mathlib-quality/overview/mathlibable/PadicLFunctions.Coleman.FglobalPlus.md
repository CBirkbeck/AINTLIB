## Statement (Phase 1)

`FglobalPlus` is **a definition** of the following:

For a fixed prime `p` and `n : ℕ`, let `ξ = ξ_{p^n}` be the chosen primitive `p^n`-th root of unity in `ℂ_p` (the p-adic complex numbers). Then `FglobalPlus p n` is the subfield `F_n⁺ = ℚ(ξ + ξ⁻¹)` of `ℂ_p` — the **maximal totally real subfield** of the cyclotomic field `F_n = ℚ(ξ_{p^n}) = ℚ(μ_{p^n})`, presented by its standard concrete generator `ξ + ξ⁻¹ = 2cos(2π/p^n)`. The module docstring cites RJW (arXiv:2309.15692) TeX 2471/2475: "`F_n⁺ = ℚ(ξ_{p^n} + ξ_{p^n}⁻¹)`: the maximal totally real subfield, rendered by its standard concrete generator."

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[hp : Fact p.Prime]` — the fixed rational prime.
- `n : ℕ` — the level in the cyclotomic tower.
- ambient field is the fixed `ℂ_[p]` (= `PadicComplex p`), with `ℚ` the base field.

Hypotheses (Lean side): none (it is an unconditional definition).

Conclusion (math): the subfield `ℚ(ξ_{p^n} + ξ_{p^n}⁻¹) ⊆ ℂ_p`.

Conclusion (Lean): `IntermediateField ℚ ℂ_[p]` (= `ℚ⟮zetaSys p n + (zetaSys p n)⁻¹⟯`).

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: **the maximal (totally) real subfield of the cyclotomic field `ℚ(ζ_n)`**, standard notations `ℚ(ζ_n)⁺`, `K_n^+`, `ℚ(μ_n)^+`.

Sources agree on the standard form: **yes** — and they agree there are *two interchangeable* standard definitions:
- **Concrete generator:** `ℚ(ζ_n)⁺ = ℚ(ζ_n + ζ_n⁻¹) = ℚ(2cos(2π/n))`. ← this is the project's `FglobalPlus`.
- **Intrinsic:** `ℚ(ζ_n)⁺ = ℚ(ζ_n) ∩ ℝ` = fixed field of complex conjugation = `{x | x̄ = x under every embedding → ℂ}`. ← this is mathlib's `NumberField.maximalRealSubfield`.

Most general standard form: the intrinsic "maximal real subfield of a number field `K`" = fixed field of complex conjugation across all embeddings `K →+* ℂ` (specializes to the cyclotomic case, where it coincides with the `ζ+ζ⁻¹` generator).

Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
- **Definition style:** concrete-generator (cyclotomic-specific) vs. intrinsic-conjugation-fixed (any number field). Both are standard; the intrinsic one is strictly more general (defined for any CM/number field, not only cyclotomic).
- **Ambient field:** the literature uniformly works inside `ℂ` (so "real" / complex conjugation is literal). **This project works inside `ℂ_p`** (p-adic), where there is no complex conjugation and no `→ ℂ` embedding — so it *must* use the concrete-generator definition; the intrinsic one is not even expressible there.

Disagreement with the literature: **none on the form itself.** `FglobalPlus = ℚ(ξ+ξ⁻¹)` is verbatim a standard definition (matches WebSearch #1, #3, nLab #6, arXiv #10). The only tension is the *ambient field*: the literature's "maximal real subfield" lives in `ℂ`; this object lives in `ℂ_p`, so the equality `ℚ(ξ+ξ⁻¹) = ℚ(ξ)∩ℝ` is not available — only the generator presentation is.

**Rationale:**
