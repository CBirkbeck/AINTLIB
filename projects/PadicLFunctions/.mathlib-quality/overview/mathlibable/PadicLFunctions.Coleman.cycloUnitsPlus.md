## Statement (Phase 1)

`PadicLFunctions.Coleman.cycloUnitsPlus p n` is **a definition** of the following.

Let `p` be a prime and `n : ℕ`. Inside the field `ℂ_[p]` of `p`-adic complex numbers
(mathlib's `PadicComplex p`), fix the primitive `p^n`-th root of unity `ξ = ξ_{p^n} =
zetaSys p n`, let `F_n = ℚ(μ_{p^n}) = ℚ(ξ)` be the cyclotomic number field and `F_n⁺ =
ℚ(ξ + ξ⁻¹)` its **maximal totally real subfield**. Let `𝒟_n = cycloUnits p n` be the group of
**cyclotomic units** of `F_n` (the intersection of the global units `O_{F_n}^×` with the
multiplicative subgroup generated by `±ξ` and the `ξ^a − 1`). Then `cycloUnitsPlus p n` is the
**plus part of the cyclotomic units**, `𝒟_n⁺ = 𝒟_n ∩ F_n⁺` — the **real cyclotomic units** —
presented as the subgroup of `ℂ_[p]ˣ` consisting of the units `u` such that (i) `u ∈ 𝒟_n` and
(ii) the value `u` lies in the real subfield `F_n⁺`.

In RJW (arXiv:2309.15692, TeX 3066) this is the `X⁺`-construction `𝒟_n⁺ = 𝒟_n ∩ F_n⁺`, the
totally-real ("plus") part of the cyclotomic units. It is one of the central objects of
cyclotomic Iwasawa theory: the real cyclotomic units `𝒟_n⁺` sit inside the full real units
`𝒱_n⁺ = O_{F_n⁺}^×` with index the plus class number `h_n⁺` (Washington Thm 8.2 / Sinnott's
index formula — the deferral the module docstring records at TeX 3072), and in this file it is
the home of the RJW generation theorem `𝒟_n⁺ = ⟨−1, γ_{n,a}⟩` (lem:cyc units gen, TeX 3461),
proved in `Generators.lean` and feeding the Iwasawa main-conjecture proof.

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[hp : Fact p.Prime]` — the prime (file scope).
- `n : ℕ` — the cyclotomic level (`F_n = ℚ(μ_{p^n})`).
- ambient: `ℂ_[p] = PadicComplex p` (normed, valued, algebraically closed, complete field).

Hypotheses (Lean side): none beyond the typeclasses; a plain `def`.

Carrier (math): `{ u ∈ ℂ_[p]ˣ : u ∈ cycloUnits p n ∧ (u : ℂ_[p]) ∈ FglobalPlus p n }`, with
the three `Subgroup` axioms (`mul_mem'`, `one_mem'`, `inv_mem'`) discharged from
`mul_mem`/`cycloUnits.inv_mem` and `IntermediateField.mul_mem`/`one_mem`/`inv_mem`.

Conclusion (math): `cycloUnitsPlus p n = 𝒟_n⁺ = 𝒟_n ∩ F_n⁺`, the real cyclotomic units of `F_n`.

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: **the plus part / "real cyclotomic units"** `𝒟_n⁺ = 𝒟_n ∩ F_n⁺` — the
cyclotomic units of `F_n = ℚ(μ_{p^n})` that lie in the maximal totally real subfield `F_n⁺ =
ℚ(ξ+ξ⁻¹)`. This is one of the central objects of cyclotomic Iwasawa theory: its index in the
full real unit group `𝒱_n⁺ = O_{F_n⁺}^×` is the plus class number `h_n⁺` (Wikipedia #9 verbatim;
Washington Thm 8.2; Sinnott's index formula `(U_F:C_F) = c_F·h_F⁺`, #3), the very index the
file's module docstring records at TeX 3072 as a permanent deferral.

Sources agree on the standard form: **yes**, and verbatim. Wikipedia (#9), Washington Ch. 8,
PlanetMath, Hida's Iwasawa notes (#2), and multiple recent arXiv papers (#1/#3/#10) all use
"real cyclotomic units = cyclotomic units in the maximal real subfield" as a named object. The
project's two carrier conditions (`u ∈ cycloUnits` = `u ∈ 𝒟_n`, and `(u:ℂ_[p]) ∈ FglobalPlus`
= `u ∈ F_n⁺`) are a **literal transcription** of "`u` is a cyclotomic unit lying in `F_n⁺`" =
`u ∈ 𝒟_n⁺`, just realised inside `ℂ_[p]`.

Most general standard form: the real cyclotomic units `C_K⁺ = C_K ∩ K⁺` for `K = ℚ(ζ_n)` of
**any** conductor `n`, stated **abstractly** as a subgroup of `(𝓞 K⁺)ˣ` (no completion / no
`ℂ_[p]` embedding); equivalently, the plus part of the abstract cyclotomic-unit group `C_K`.

Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
- **conductor**: prime-power `p^n` (the file) ⊂ any `n` (the full standard form). For composite
  `n` the underlying `ζ_n^a − 1` are themselves units, so the *parent* group's generators differ
  (this is inherited from `cycloUnits`; the `∩ K⁺` plus-part operation is conductor-uniform).
- **ambient**: *abstract* `(𝓞 K⁺)ˣ` / `C_K⁺ ≤ (𝓞 K)ˣ` (the universal literature choice) vs.
  *embedded in `ℂ_[p]`* (the file's RJW "everything inside `ℂ_p`" convention).
- **"real" presentation**: *intrinsic* (fixed field of complex conjugation, needs `K →+* ℂ`) vs.
  *concrete generator* `ℚ(ξ+ξ⁻¹)` (the only form available in `ℂ_p` — same axis as `FglobalPlus`).

Disagreement with the literature: **none mathematically.** The project's definition is the
standard "real cyclotomic units" specialised to prime-power conductor and re-presented inside
`ℂ_[p]`.
