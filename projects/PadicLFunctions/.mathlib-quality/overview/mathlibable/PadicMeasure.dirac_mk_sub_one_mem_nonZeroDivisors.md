### Statement (Phase 1)

`PadicMeasure.dirac_mk_sub_one_mem_nonZeroDivisors` is **a theorem** stating the following:

Let `p` be an odd prime and let `Λ(𝒢) := PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ` be the Iwasawa
(measure) algebra of `ℤ_[p]ˣ` — concretely the convolution ring of `ℤ_[p]`-linear
functionals `C(ℤ_[p]ˣ, ℤ_[p]) →ₗ[ℤ_[p]] ℤ_[p]`. Let `π : ℤ_[p]ˣ → 𝒢⁺ := ℤ_[p]ˣ/{±1}`
be the quotient by `{±1}`, and `π_* : Λ(𝒢) → Λ(𝒢⁺)` the induced ring map on measure
algebras. The theorem says: if the augmentation-style element `[a] − [1]` (the
difference of the Dirac measure at `a` and the unit) is a **non-zero-divisor** in
`Λ(𝒢)`, then its image `[ā] − [1]` is a **non-zero-divisor** in `Λ(𝒢⁺)`, where
`ā = π(a)`. In words: *regularity of the augmentation generator `[a]−1` descends along
the group quotient `ℤ_[p]ˣ ↠ ℤ_[p]ˣ/{±1}`.*

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[hp : Fact p.Prime]` — the residue characteristic.
- `{a : ℤ_[p]ˣ}` — a unit of `ℤ_p`, mathematically a topological generator of the
  procyclic part used downstream.

Hypotheses (Lean side):
- `hp2 : p ≠ 2` — oddness; needed because the proof rests on the `Λ(𝒢) = Λ(𝒢)⁺ ⊕ Λ(𝒢)⁻`
  decomposition, which requires `2` invertible in `ℤ_[p]`.
- `ha : (dirac p a − 1) ∈ nonZeroDivisors (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ)` — the 𝒢-side
  regularity hypothesis.

Conclusion (math): `[ā] − [1]` is a non-zero-divisor in the quotient-group measure algebra `Λ(𝒢⁺)`.

Conclusion (Lean): `(dirac p (QuotientGroup.mk a : GPlus p) − 1 : PadicMeasure p (GPlus p)) ∈ nonZeroDivisors (PadicMeasure p (GPlus p))`.
