### Statement (Phase 1)

`PadicMeasure.zetaIdeal` is **a definition of the ideal `I(𝒢)ζ_p`** of the cyclotomic
Iwasawa algebra `Λ(𝒢) = Λ(ℤ_p^×)`:

> Let `Λ(𝒢)` be the Iwasawa algebra of `𝒢 = ℤ_p^×` (here realised concretely as the
> algebra of `ℤ_p`-valued measures `C(ℤ_p^×, ℤ_p) →ₗ[ℤ_p] ℤ_p`, RJW Def. 3.6), `Q(𝒢)`
> its total ring of fractions (RJW Def. 3.34), `I(𝒢) = ker(deg)` the augmentation ideal
> (RJW Def. 3.37), and `ζ_p ∈ Q(𝒢)` the Kubota–Leopoldt p-adic zeta pseudo-measure
> (RJW Def. 4.10). Then `I(𝒢)ζ_p := { x ∈ Λ(𝒢) : x = λ·ζ_p in Q(𝒢) for some λ ∈ I(𝒢) }`
> is an ideal of `Λ(𝒢)`. (The pseudo-measure property — `([g]−[1])·ζ_p ∈ Λ(𝒢)` for all
> `g` — is precisely what makes the products `λ·ζ_p` land back in `Λ(𝒢)`, so the set is a
> genuine subset of `Λ(𝒢)`, and the augmentation-ideal/ring structure make it an ideal.)

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):
- `p : ℕ`, `[hp : Fact p.Prime]` — the prime.
- `hp2 : p ≠ 2` — odd prime (essential: `ℤ_p^×` needs a topological generator; `(ℤ/8)^×` is not cyclic). Enters only because `padicZeta p hp2` requires it.
- implicit: the existing `CommRing (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ)` instance (Iwasawa-algebra ring structure) and the `FractionRing`/`IsFractionRing` structure on `QuotientField p`.

Hypotheses (Lean side): none beyond `hp2`. The carrier is the membership predicate
`∃ l ∈ augmentationIdeal p (G := ℤ_[p]ˣ), algebraMap _ (QuotientField p) x = algebraMap _ _ l * padicZeta p hp2`.

Conclusion (math): `I(𝒢)ζ_p` is an ideal of the Iwasawa algebra `Λ(𝒢)`.

Conclusion (Lean): `Ideal (PadicMeasure p ℤ_[p]ˣ)` — n/a, this is a definition (the four `Ideal` fields).

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: **`I(G)ζ_p`** — "the ideal of the Iwasawa algebra generated by
the (augmentation ideal times the) p-adic zeta pseudo-measure"; the **right-hand side of
Iwasawa's Main Conjecture** in the cyclotomic case. Equivalent names: "the ideal `Iζ`",
"the ζ_p-ideal", "the characteristic-ideal side of the main conjecture".
Sources agree on the standard form: **yes** — Coates–Sujatha *Cyclotomic Fields and Zeta
Values* (Ch. 4), RJW arXiv:2309.15692 (§11.2, the Proposition at TeX 3052), Sharifi's AWS
notes, Ouyang's lecture notes. The definition is uniformly: take `λ` in the augmentation
ideal `I(G)`, multiply by the pseudo-measure `ζ_p` in the total fraction ring; the
products lie in `Λ(G)` (pseudo-measure property) and form an ideal.
Most general standard form: for **any** profinite group `G` (e.g. `G = Gal(ℚ(μ_{p^∞})/ℚ)`,
or its even part `G⁺`) admitting a pseudo-measure `ζ ∈ Q(G)`, `I(G)ζ` is an ideal of
`Λ(G)`. The cyclotomic `G = ℤ_p^×` is the principal case.
Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
  - **the group `G`**: ranges from the concrete cyclotomic `ℤ_p^×` / `Gal` group to an
    arbitrary profinite (often pro-`p` or commutative `p`-adic Lie) group `G`. The Lean
    def fixes `G = ℤ_p^×`; the literature's general statement is "any `G` with a pseudo-
    measure". (`zetaIdealPlus` in the same file is the `G⁺` instance — so the project
    *already* duplicates the construction for a second group, which is itself a signal the
    general-`G` form is the natural target.)
  - **the pseudo-measure `ζ`**: from the specific Kubota–Leopoldt `ζ_p` to an arbitrary
    pseudo-measure `q ∈ Q(G)`. The construction `I(G)·q` makes sense for any pseudo-measure.
Disagreement with the literature: **none**. The Lean form is a faithful, correctly-stated
specialisation of the standard `I(G)ζ_p`.

### Composition check (Phase 6)

Can `zetaIdeal` be derived from mathlib in ≤3 chained calls?

Attempt 1 (abstract `pseudoMeasureIdeal` as a mathlib composition): is
`{x | ∃ l ∈ I, algebraMap x = algebraMap l * q}` expressible as e.g.
`(I.map (algebraMap Λ Q) * q-stuff).comap (algebraMap Λ Q)` or similar?

    scalar-multiplication of a submodule by an element.
  - Result: **fails** — there is no single mathlib operation "ideal `I` of `Λ`, scaled by a
    *fixed element `q` of the fraction ring `Q`*, then pulled back to `Λ`". `q` is not an
    element of `Λ`, so `I * (q)` is not an ideal operation inside `Λ`; the pull-back of the
