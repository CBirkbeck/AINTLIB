### Statement (Phase 1)

`WeierstrassCurve.preΨ_odd` is the **odd-index duplication recurrence** for the auxiliary
univariate division polynomials `preΨ` of a Weierstrass curve `W` over a commutative ring `R`.
For every `m : ℤ`,

  preΨ(2m+1) = preΨ(m+2)·preΨ(m)³·(Ψ₂Sq² if m even, else 1)
             − preΨ(m−1)·preΨ(m+1)³·(1 if m even, else Ψ₂Sq²).

Mathematically this is the standard odd-term recursion of an elliptic divisibility sequence
(Ward's recurrence ψ_{2n+1} = ψ_{n+2}ψ_n³ − ψ_{n−1}ψ_{n+1}³), specialised to the polynomial
EDS generated by `(Ψ₂Sq², Ψ₃, preΨ₄)`. The `if Even m` split is bookkeeping that pushes the
ψ₂² factors onto the correct parity so the recursion stays inside the *normalised* (square-free
of ψ₂) `preΨ` track.

Variables / typeclasses (Lean side):
- `{R : Type r} [CommRing R]` — base ring.
- `(W : WeierstrassCurve R)` — the curve (supplies `Ψ₂Sq`, `Ψ₃`, `preΨ₄`, `preΨ`).
- `(m : ℤ)` — the index.

Hypotheses: none.

Conclusion (Lean): an equation in `R[X]` (`Polynomial R`) — `W.preΨ (2 * m + 1) = …`.

It is a thin restatement of the underlying EDS identity `preNormEDS_odd` proved by `preNormEDS_odd ..`.
