### Statement (Phase 1)

`shortCurveQ_a₁` is a coefficient-extraction lemma. For integers `A B : ℤ`, it
states that the first Weierstrass coefficient `a₁` of the rational short
Weierstrass curve `shortCurveQ A B` equals `0`.

Here `shortCurveQ A B := (shortCurveZ A B).map (algebraMap ℤ ℚ)` (line 29–30),
and `shortCurveZ A B` is the integral curve `⟨a₁:=0, a₂:=0, a₃:=0, a₄:=A,
a₆:=B⟩` (line 25–26). So mathematically the claim is: base-changing the short
curve `y² = x³ + Ax + B` from ℤ to ℚ leaves `a₁ = 0` (it does, because base
change applies `algebraMap` coefficient-wise and `algebraMap ℤ ℚ 0 = 0`).

Variables / typeclasses involved (Lean side):

Hypotheses (Lean side): none.

Conclusion (math): the `a₁`-coefficient of the ℚ-base-changed short curve is 0.

coefficients map through any ring homomorphism). The specific lemma is the
`a₁`-component, specialised to the short model where `a₁ = 0`.
Sources agree on the standard form: yes — base change is coefficient-wise; this
is definitional.
Most general standard form: for any ring hom `f : R →+* A` and curve `W`,
`(W.map f).a₁ = f W.a₁`. The lemma here is the `R=ℤ, A=ℚ, f=algebraMap,
W=shortCurveZ A B` instance, further simplified by `f 0 = 0`.
