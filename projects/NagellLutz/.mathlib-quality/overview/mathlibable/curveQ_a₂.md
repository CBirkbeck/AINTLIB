## 1. Mathematical content

`curveQ_a₂` states that the `a₂` Weierstrass coefficient of the base change of an integral
Weierstrass curve `W` to `ℚ` equals `(W.a₂ : ℚ)`. This is a pure structure-projection identity:
`map` is defined componentwise (`⟨f a₁, f a₂, f a₃, f a₄, f a₆⟩`), so reading off the `a₂` field
just returns `f W.a₂`, and here `f = algebraMap ℤ ℚ`, which on `ℤ` is the integer-cast coercion.
There is no number theory and no elliptic-curve geometry in the statement — it is bookkeeping that
lets downstream files rewrite `(curveQ W).a₂ ⇝ (W.a₂ : ℚ)` under `simp`.
