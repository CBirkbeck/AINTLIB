### Statement (Phase 1)

`evalEval_ω` is a **naturality / base-change compatibility lemma**. Exact statement:

```lean
lemma evalEval_ω : (W.ω n).evalEval x y = polyEval W x y (curve.ω n) := by
  simp_rw [polyEval_apply, ← map_ω, map_specialize]
```

In words: for a Weierstrass curve `W` over a commutative ring `R`, a point `(x, y)`
over `R`, and `n : ℤ`, **evaluating the omega division polynomial `ωₙ` of `W` at
`(x, y)` equals applying the universal-curve→`R` evaluation ring homomorphism
`polyEval` to the universal omega polynomial `curve.ω n`.**

Here `curve : Affine (MvPolynomial Coeff ℤ)` is the *universal* Weierstrass curve over
`ℤ[A₁,A₂,A₃,A₄,A₆]` (`Universal.lean:84`), and `polyEval : Poly →+* R` is the ring
hom that specialises the indeterminates `Aᵢ ↦ aᵢ` (via `specialize`) then evaluates
`X ↦ x, Y ↦ y` (`Universal.lean:203`). The proof is three rewrites: `polyEval_apply`
(unfold `polyEval` as `evalEval`-after-`map`), `← map_ω` (naturality of `ω` under
ring homs, `DivisionPolynomialOmega.lean:111`), and `map_specialize` (`W` is the
specialisation of the universal curve, `Universal.lean:194`).

Variables / typeclasses:
- `{R : Type*} [CommRing R]`, `W : WeierstrassCurve R` — base curve.
- `{x y : R}` — coordinates of a point in the affine plane over `R`.
- `{n : ℤ}` — the division-polynomial index.

Hypotheses: none (pure identity, holds for all `W, x, y, n`).

Conclusion (math): evaluation of `ωₙ` at `(x,y)` factors through the universal
omega polynomial via `polyEval`.
Conclusion (Lean): `(W.ω n).evalEval x y = polyEval W x y (curve.ω n)` — an equation
in `R`.

### Literature summary (Phase 3)

Concept identified as: **the omega (`ωₙ`) family of Weierstrass division polynomials**
(classical; Silverman *AEC* III.§3; the doubling-`Y`-coordinate polynomial), together
with a **naturality/base-change bridge** to the universal curve.
Sources agree on the standard form of `ωₙ`: **yes** — `ωₙ := (ψ₂ₙ/ψₙ − ψₙ(a₁φₙ +
a₃ψₙ²))/2`, well-defined as a polynomial; this is exactly the formula in mathlib's own
docstring (`DivisionPolynomial/Basic.lean:30`).
Most general standard form of the *bridge* statement: "evaluation of a division
polynomial at a point factors through the universal coefficient ring via the
specialization homomorphism" — i.e. a special case of *the value of a universal
polynomial construction commutes with specialization*. This is folklore bookkeeping,
**not a named theorem**.
Generality dimensions where the literature varies:
  - base structure: `ωₙ` makes sense over any commutative ring (mathlib's intended
    generality); char ≠ 2 only needed to *divide by 2*, but the polynomial itself is
    integral. The project's `ω` (DivisionPolynomialOmega.lean:74) is over any `CommRing R`. ✓ maximal.
  - the bridge: stated for `evalEval`-at-a-point; the underlying principle generalises
    to any ring hom (which is precisely `map_ω`, already separate).
Disagreement with the literature: none. The Lean form *is* the construction the
mathlib docstring proposes.

**Key literature finding:** mathlib's `DivisionPolynomial/Basic.lean` module docstring
(lines 28–38, 71, 83) **explicitly lists `ωₙ` as a TODO** and describes the universal-
morphism definition this project implements verbatim:
> "* TODO: the bivariate polynomials `ωₙ`." (line 71)
> "`ωₙ` can be equivalently defined as the image of this division under the associated
> universal morphism `𝓡[X, Y] → R[X, Y]` mapping `Aᵢ` to `aᵢ`." (lines 37–38)
