### `theorem cyclic_subgroup_meets_G_times_one_trivially`
- **Type**: `(G H : Type*) [Group G] [Group H] [Finite G] [Finite H] (σ : G) (τ : H) (_hn : Nat.card G ∣ orderOf τ) : (Subgroup.zpowers (σ, τ)) ⊓ ((⊤ : Subgroup G).prod (⊥ : Subgroup H)) = ⊥`
- **What**: In `G × H`, if `|G| ∣ ord τ` then the cyclic subgroup generated by `(σ, τ)` meets the subgroup `G × {1}` trivially.
- **How**: Take `(g,h)` in the meet; membership in `G × {1}` forces `h = 1`, and being a power `(σ,τ)^k` gives `τ^k = 1`, so `ord τ ∣ k`, hence `|G| ∣ k` (via `_hn`) hence `σ^k = 1` (via `orderOf_dvd_natCard`); thus `(g,h) = 1`. Sharifi sub-lemma (i).
- **Hypotheses**: `G`, `H` finite groups; `|G|` divides the order of `τ`.

- **Used by**: unused in file (the concrete `Gal(M/K)` realisation is `zpowers_inf_fixingSubgroup_eq_bot_aux`, which re-derives the same fact directly; cited in docstrings of C3 but not invoked).
